# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful, harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of experience level, gender, sexual orientation, disability, ethnicity, religion or nationality.

## Our standards

Behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experience
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Behaviour that is not acceptable:

- Harassment, insults or derogatory comments in any form
- Personal or political attacks
- Publishing others' private information without permission
- Any other conduct that would be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behaviour may be reported to **conduct@incubacloud.io**. All reports will be reviewed and investigated promptly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality regarding the reporter.
